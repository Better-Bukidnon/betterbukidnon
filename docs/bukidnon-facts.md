# Bukidnon Fact Sheet

The single source of truth for province-level facts shown on BetterBukidnon.org. When a
page, data file or translation states a fact about Bukidnon, it should match this file.
When a fact changes (new census, new officials, new hotline), update this file first and
then the pages listed under "Where it appears".

Every entry carries a **status**:

- **Verified**: confirmed against the cited source.
- **Verify**: taken from a secondary source or an older directory. Confirm it with the
  Provincial Government of Bukidnon before treating it as authoritative.

## 5. History (for the homepage timeline)

| Year         | Event                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pre-colonial | The highlands are home to the Bukidnon, Higaonon, Talaandig, Manobo, Matigsalug, Tigwahanon and Umayamnon peoples.                        |
| 1850s        | Under Spanish rule the area becomes part of the province of Misamis.                                                                      |
| 1907         | Act No. 1693 (20 August) creates the province of Agusan, with Bukidnon as a sub-province. Malaybalay becomes its capital on 23 September. |
| 1914         | Act No. 2408 (1 September) organizes the Department of Mindanao and Sulu; Bukidnon is constituted as a province within it.                |
| 1917         | Act No. 2711 (10 March) makes Bukidnon a regular province. 10 March is celebrated as Bukidnon's founding anniversary.                     |
| 1942–1945    | Japanese occupation. Bukidnon-based guerrillas help Filipino and American forces liberate the province.                                   |
| 1977         | The first organized Kaamulan Festival is held (25–27 November), sponsored by the provincial government.                                   |
| 1998         | Malaybalay becomes a component city (Republic Act No. 8490, 22 March).                                                                    |
| 2001         | Valencia becomes a component city.                                                                                                        |
| 2012         | Republic Act No. 10184 reapportions Bukidnon into four legislative districts, in effect from the 2013 elections.                          |

Kaamulan comes from the Binukid word _amul_, "to gather". The festival gathers the seven
tribes to give thanks to _Magbabaya_ for the harvest, and runs in Malaybalay around the
10 March founding anniversary.

## 6. Economy and landmarks

- Known as the "food basket of Mindanao": rice, corn, sugarcane, coffee, rubber,
  pineapple, banana, vegetables, cut flowers, cassava; poultry, hogs and cattle.
- Del Monte Philippines' pineapple plantation in Manolo Fortich; Dole, San Miguel Foods
  and other agro-industrial operations.
- Mount Kitanglad Range Natural Park and Mount Kalatungan; Pulangi River.
- Central Mindanao University (Musuan, Maramag) and Bukidnon State University
  (Malaybalay).
