## About the Office

What the Provincial Health Office is for

The Provincial Health Office is headed by the provincial health officer, a position the Local Government Code (Republic Act 7160) requires in every province. The office plans and carries out the province's health programs, advises the Governor and the Sangguniang Panlalawigan on health matters, and supervises the health officers of the cities and municipalities in Bukidnon.

### General responsibilities

<Requirements>

- Plans and runs provincial health programs
- Advises the Governor and the Sangguniang Panlalawigan
- Supervises city and municipal health officers
- Works with the Department of Health on national programs

</Requirements>

## Where to Get Care

Everyday health services are closest to home

<Steps>

1. **Barangay health station**: For basic check-ups and health concerns in your community.
2. **City or municipal health office**: Your city or municipal health office (rural health unit) handles consultations, immunization and other public health services in your area.
3. **Emergencies**: Call 911 or go to the nearest hospital.

</Steps>
