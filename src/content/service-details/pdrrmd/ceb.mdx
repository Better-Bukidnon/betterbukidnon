## Mahitungod sa Departamento

Ang opisina sa probinsya alang sa disaster risk reduction

Republic Act 10121, the Philippine Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act of 2010, requires every province, city and municipality to have a local disaster risk reduction and management office and council. In Bukidnon, the provincial office is the PDRRMD. The provincial council is chaired by the Governor.

Under the law, local DRRM offices assess hazards and risks, run early warning and public information, train and organize emergency response teams and volunteers, and coordinate the local response when a disaster strikes.

## Sa Emerhensya

Unsay buhaton ug kinsay tawgan

<Steps>

1. **Tawag sa 911**: ### Tawag sa 911 Alang sa bisan unsang emerhensya nga makamatay, tawga ang National Emergency Hotline.
2. **Kontaka ang imong lokal nga DRRM office**: ### Kontaka ang imong lokal nga DRRM office Your barangay and your city or municipal DRRM office are usually the first responders in your area. Report disaster-related incidents to the PDRRMD at (088) 813-1262.
3. **Sunda ang opisyal nga mga advisory**: ### Sunda ang opisyal nga mga advisory Follow weather and earthquake advisories from PAGASA and PHIVOLCS, and instructions from your local officials, including evacuation orders.
4. **Ilha ang imong evacuation center**: ### Ilha ang imong evacuation center Pangutana sa imong barangay kung asa ang imong evacuation center ug unsaon pag-adto sa wala pa nimo kini kinahanglan.

</Steps>
