## About the Office

What the Provincial Engineer does under the law

<Service>

The Provincial Engineer's Office (PEO) is the engineering and public works office of the Province of Bukidnon. Under the Local Government Code of 1991 (Republic Act No. 7160), the Provincial Engineer plans, designs, supervises and maintains the infrastructure of the province, such as provincial roads, bridges and buildings, and gives technical help to the cities and municipalities.

The PEO does not handle every road or building in Bukidnon. Building permits, local streets and barangay roads belong to your city or municipality, and national roads belong to the DPWH.

</Service>

## How to Reach the Office

General steps. See the Citizen's Charter for official steps.

<ServiceList>

<Requirements title={"Steps"}>

1. Find out who maintains the road or facility. Provincial roads are the PEO's concern; national roads, city or municipal streets and barangay roads are not.
2. Call the PEO or visit the office at the Capitol. Describe the location and the problem, with a landmark or a photo if you have one.
3. Ask how your report or request will be handled and whether it needs a letter.
4. Keep the name of the person you spoke to and the date, in case you need to follow up.

</Requirements>

<Requirements title={"What to Bring"}>

- A valid government-issued ID
- Exact location, with landmarks, of the road, bridge or project
- A written letter for formal requests, such as technical assistance
- Requirements may vary; confirm with the office.

</Requirements>

</ServiceList>

## Handled by Your City or Municipality

These are not provincial engineering services

<ServiceList>

<Service title={"Building Permits"}>

Building permits are issued by the Office of the Building Official of your city or municipality, under the National Building Code.

</Service>

<Service title={"Local Streets and Barangay Roads"}>

Streets and barangay roads are maintained by the city, municipality or barangay.

</Service>

<Service title={"National Roads"}>

National roads and bridges are under the DPWH, not the Province. Contact the DPWH district engineering office that covers your area.

</Service>

</ServiceList>
