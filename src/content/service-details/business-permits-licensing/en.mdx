<Callout title={"Who issues business permits"}>

The Provincial Government of Bukidnon does not issue business permits for stores, offices or other establishments. Permits (often called the Mayor's Permit) are issued by the Business Permits and Licensing Office (BPLO) of the city or municipality where the business is located: Malaybalay City, Valencia City, or one of the 20 municipalities. Requirements, fees and deadlines vary, so confirm with that office. [Find your city or municipality](/government#lgus)

</Callout>

## Commonly Asked Requirements

Each city and municipality publishes its own list. Use these to prepare, then confirm with your BPLO.

<ServiceList>

<Requirements title={"New Business"}>

- Duly accomplished Application Form
- DTI Certificate of Business Name Registration (for sole proprietorship)
- SEC Registration (for corporation/partnership)
- CDA Registration (for cooperatives)
- Barangay Business Clearance
- Proof of business location, such as a lease contract, or the title or tax declaration if you own the place
- Valid ID of the owner or authorized person

</Requirements>

<Requirements title={"Renewal"}>

- Previous year's business permit
- Declaration or proof of gross sales or receipts for the past year
- Barangay business clearance for the current year
- Updated clearances your city or municipality asks for

</Requirements>

</ServiceList>

## What the Provincial Government Handles

<Requirements>

- Under the Local Government Code, permits to extract sand, gravel and other quarry resources are issued by the Provincial Governor.
- Provincial taxes and fees are paid at the Provincial Treasurer's Office. [Provincial Treasurer's Office](/service-details/provincial-treasurer)

</Requirements>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<ServiceList>

<Service title={"When do I renew my business permit?"}>

Every year. Under the Local Government Code, local business taxes are due in January, with quarterly installments where the LGU allows them. Check your city or municipality's deadline and penalties.

</Service>

<Service title={"Can I apply online?"}>

Some cities and municipalities accept online applications through their own systems or an electronic Business One-Stop Shop. Ask your BPLO what it offers.

</Service>

<Service title={"My business has branches in different towns."}>

Each city or municipality issues its own permit. A branch or outlet usually needs a permit from the LGU where it operates.

</Service>

<Service title={"Where do I register my business name?"}>

Sole proprietors register the business name with the DTI. Corporations and partnerships register with the SEC, and cooperatives with the CDA.

</Service>

</ServiceList>
