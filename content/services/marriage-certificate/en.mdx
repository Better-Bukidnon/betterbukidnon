<Notice icon="info-circle" title="Who handles marriage records">
  Marriage licenses and marriage registration are handled by the Local Civil Registry Office (LCRO)
  of each city and municipality: Malaybalay City, Valencia City, or one of the 20 municipalities of
  Bukidnon. The Provincial Government does not issue marriage licenses or marriage certificates.
  Requirements and fees vary by city and municipality, so confirm with the office before you go.
  [Find your city or municipality](/government#lgus)
</Notice>

</Section>

<Section alt icon="files" title="Two Ways to Get a Copy" intro="Choose the office copy or the PSA copy, depending on what the requesting agency needs">

<RouteGrid>

<ListBox icon="building" title="From the City or Municipal Civil Registrar">

1. Go to the civil registrar of the city or municipality where the marriage was registered (where the wedding took place).
2. Fill out the request form: names of both spouses and the date and place of the marriage.
3. Present a valid ID. A representative also brings an authorization letter and the spouse's ID.
4. Pay the fee set by the LGU and claim the certified copy.

</ListBox>

<ListBox icon="globe" title="From the PSA">

1. Order online through PSA Serbilis for delivery, or visit a PSA Civil Registry System outlet.
2. Give the full names of both spouses and the date and place of the marriage.
3. Present a valid ID if you go in person, and pay the PSA fee.
4. Receive the copy printed on PSA security paper. The PSA also issues the Certificate of No Marriage Record (CENOMAR).

<ListNote>[PSA Serbilis (online requests)](https://www.psaserbilis.com.ph/)</ListNote>

</ListBox>

</RouteGrid>

<Section icon="list-ol" title="Getting a Marriage License and Registering the Marriage" intro="General steps. Your civil registrar may ask for more.">

<ProcessCards>
  <ProcessCard title="Apply for a Marriage License">
    Apply together at the civil registrar of the city or municipality where either of you usually
    lives.
  </ProcessCard>
  <ProcessCard title="Attend Pre-Marriage Counseling">
    Attend the pre-marriage orientation or counseling the civil registrar requires and get the
    certificate.
  </ProcessCard>
  <ProcessCard title="Wait for the 10-Day Posting">
    The notice of your application is posted for 10 consecutive days before the license is released.
  </ProcessCard>
  <ProcessCard title="Receive the License">
    The marriage license is valid anywhere in the Philippines for 120 days from the date it is
    issued.
  </ProcessCard>
  <ProcessCard title="Hold the Wedding">
    The couple, the solemnizing officer and at least two witnesses sign the Certificate of Marriage.
  </ProcessCard>
  <ProcessCard title="Registration" final>
    The solemnizing officer files the Certificate of Marriage with the civil registrar of the place
    of the wedding, usually within 15 days. Follow up to make sure it was registered.
  </ProcessCard>
</ProcessCards>

<ListBox icon="file-earmark-text" title="For a Marriage License">

- PSA birth certificates of both parties
- PSA Certificate of No Marriage Record (CENOMAR) of both parties
- Valid IDs of both parties
- Certificate of pre-marriage counseling or orientation
- Parental consent if a party is 18 to 20 years old; parental advice if 21 to 24

</ListBox>

<ListBox icon="person-exclamation" title="In Special Cases">

- Widowed: death certificate of the previous spouse
- Annulled or nullified marriage: court decision, certificate of finality and the annotated marriage certificate
- Foreign national: certificate of legal capacity to contract marriage from their embassy or consulate

</ListBox>

<SectionHeading icon="question-circle" title="Frequently Asked Questions" />

<Faq question="Where do we apply for a marriage license?">
  At the civil registrar of the city or municipality where either of you usually lives. The license
  can be used for a wedding anywhere in the Philippines within 120 days.
</Faq>

<Faq question="Who registers the marriage after the wedding?">
  The solemnizing officer files the Certificate of Marriage with the civil registrar of the city or
  municipality where the wedding was held. Ask the officer or the civil registrar to confirm that it
  was registered.
</Faq>

<Faq question="Where do I get a CENOMAR?">
  Only the PSA issues the Certificate of No Marriage Record (CENOMAR). Request it online through PSA
  Serbilis or at a PSA outlet.
</Faq>

<Faq question="Why can't the PSA find our marriage certificate?">
  A newly registered record takes time to reach the PSA. Ask the civil registrar where the marriage
  was registered to confirm that the record was sent, or to endorse it to the PSA.
</Faq>

<Faq question="How much are the fees?">
  Each city and municipality sets its own fees for marriage licenses and local copies, and the PSA
  sets its own fees. Ask the office for the current amounts.
</Faq>
