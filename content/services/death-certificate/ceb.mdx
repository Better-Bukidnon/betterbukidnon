<Notice icon="info-circle" title="Kinsay nag-asikaso sa death certificate">
  Deaths are registered with the Local Civil Registry Office (LCRO) of the city or municipality
  where the death occurred: Malaybalay City, Valencia City, or one of the 20 municipalities of
  Bukidnon. This may not be the town where the person lived. The Provincial Government does not
  register deaths or issue death certificates. Requirements and fees vary by city and municipality,
  so confirm with the office before you go. [Pangitaa ang imong siyudad o
  munisipyo](/government#lgus)
</Notice>

<Section alt icon="list-ol" title="Pagrehistro og Kamatayon" intro="Kinatibuk-ang mga lakang. Mahimong mangayo og dugang ang imong civil registrar.">

<ProcessCards>
  <ProcessCard title="Kuhaa ang Certificate of Death">
    The attending physician fills out and signs the medical part of the Certificate of Death. In a
    hospital, the hospital usually prepares it.
  </ProcessCard>
  <ProcessCard title="Walay Attending Physician?">
    If the person died at home without a doctor, the city or municipal health officer examines the
    case and certifies the cause of death.
  </ProcessCard>
  <ProcessCard title="Pirmahan sa Informant">
    A family member or other informant checks the personal details, signs, and presents a valid ID.
  </ProcessCard>
  <ProcessCard title="Isumite Sulod sa 30 Ka Adlaw">
    File the Certificate of Death with the civil registrar of the city or municipality where the
    death occurred, within 30 days.
  </ProcessCard>
  <ProcessCard title="Kuhaa ang Burial Permit">
    The city or municipality issues the burial permit, and a transfer permit if the remains will be
    moved to another town. Funeral homes often help with this.
  </ProcessCard>
  <ProcessCard title="Kuhaa ang Imong mga Kopya" final>
    The civil registrar can issue a local copy once the death is registered. The record is then sent
    to the PSA, so a PSA copy is available only after some time.
  </ProcessCard>
</ProcessCards>

</Section>

<Section icon="files" title="Duha ka Paagi sa Pagkuha og Kopya" intro="Pilia ang kopya sa opisina o ang kopya sa PSA, depende sa gikinahanglan sa ahensya nga nagpangayo">

<RouteGrid>

<ListBox icon="building" title="Gikan sa City o Municipal Civil Registrar">

1. Adtoa ang civil registrar sa siyudad o munisipyo diin narehistro ang kamatayon.
2. Pun-a ang request form: tibuok nga ngalan sa namatay ug ang petsa ug lugar sa kamatayon.
3. Ipakita ang balidong ID. Ang representante magdala usab og sulat sa pagtugot.
4. Bayri ang bayranan nga gitakda sa LGU ug kuhaa ang certified copy.

</ListBox>

<ListBox icon="globe" title="Gikan sa PSA">

1. Order online through PSA Serbilis for delivery, or visit a PSA Civil Registry System outlet.
2. Ihatag ang tibuok nga ngalan sa namatay ug ang petsa ug lugar sa kamatayon.
3. Ipakita ang balidong ID kung moadto ka personal, ug bayri ang bayranan sa PSA.
4. Madawat ang kopya nga giimprenta sa PSA security paper.

<ListNote>[PSA Serbilis (online nga hangyo)](https://www.psaserbilis.com.ph/)</ListNote>

</ListBox>

<ListBox icon="file-earmark-text" title="Alang sa Pagrehistro Sulod sa 30 Ka Adlaw">

- Certificate of Death signed by the attending physician or the city or municipal health officer
- Balidong ID sa informant
- Uban pang dokumento nga gipangayo sa civil registrar, depende sa kaso

</ListBox>

<ListBox icon="clock-history" title="Alang sa Delayed Registration (Human sa 30 Ka Adlaw)">

- Affidavit nga nagpatin-aw sa hinungdan sa paglangan
- Negative certification sa PSA
- Supporting documents, sama sa burial o cemetery certificate

</ListBox>

<SectionHeading icon="question-circle" title="Mga Kasagarang Pangutana" />

<Faq question="Namatay ang tawo sa ospital sa laing lungsod. Asa mi magrehistro?">
  At the civil registrar of the city or municipality where the death occurred, which is where the
  hospital is, not where the person lived.
</Faq>

<Faq question="Nag-isyu ba ang Provincial Capitol og death certificate?">
  No. Death records are kept by the civil registrar of the city or municipality where the death was
  registered, and by the PSA.
</Faq>

<Faq question="Pila ang bayad?">
  Each city and municipality sets its own fees for registration, burial permits and local copies,
  and the PSA sets its own fees. Ask the office for the current amounts.
</Faq>

<Faq question="Wala pay kopya ang PSA. Unsa mi nay buhaton?">
  A newly registered record takes time to reach the PSA. Ask the civil registrar where the death was
  registered to confirm that the record was sent, or to endorse it to the PSA.
</Faq>

<Faq question="Unsaon nako pagtul-id sa sayop sa death certificate?">
  Clerical or typographical errors can be corrected by petition at the civil registrar under
  Republic Act 9048. Other changes need a court order.
</Faq>
