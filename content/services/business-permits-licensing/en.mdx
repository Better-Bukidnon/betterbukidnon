<Notice icon="info-circle" title="Who issues business permits">
  The Provincial Government of Bukidnon does not issue business permits for stores, offices or other
  establishments. Permits (often called the Mayor's Permit) are issued by the Business Permits and
  Licensing Office (BPLO) of the city or municipality where the business is located: Malaybalay
  City, Valencia City, or one of the 20 municipalities. Requirements, fees and deadlines vary, so
  confirm with that office. [Find your city or municipality](/government#lgus)
</Notice>

</Section>

<Section alt icon="list-ol" title="Getting a New Business Permit" intro="The usual order of steps. Your city or municipality may combine some of them in a Business One-Stop Shop.">

<ProcessTable widths="34,40,26">

| Step                                                             | What to Do                                                                                                                                                    | Where                                           |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| <StepBadge>1</StepBadge> **Register the business**               | Register the business name (sole proprietorship), the corporation or partnership, or the cooperative.                                                         | DTI, SEC, or CDA                                |
| <StepBadge>2</StepBadge> **Get a barangay business clearance**   | Apply for a clearance from the barangay where the business will operate.                                                                                      | Barangay hall                                   |
| <StepBadge>3</StepBadge> **Apply for the business permit**       | Submit the application form and requirements. The office assesses your fees and local business taxes.                                                         | City or municipal BPLO                          |
| <StepBadge>4</StepBadge> **Pay fees and taxes**                  | Pay the assessed fees and local business taxes and keep the official receipt.                                                                                 | City or municipal treasurer's office            |
| <StepBadge>5</StepBadge> **Complete clearances and inspections** | Depending on the business, this can include a fire safety inspection, a sanitary permit, a zoning or locational clearance, and building or occupancy permits. | BFP and the city or municipal offices concerned |
| <StepBadge>6</StepBadge> **Claim and display the permit**        | Claim the business permit and display it where customers can see it.                                                                                          | City or municipal BPLO                          |
| <StepBadge>7</StepBadge> **Register with the BIR**               | Register for tax purposes with the BIR Revenue District Office that covers your business address.                                                             | BIR                                             |

</ProcessTable>

</Section>

<Section icon="clipboard-check" title="Commonly Asked Requirements" intro="Each city and municipality publishes its own list. Use these to prepare, then confirm with your BPLO.">

<RouteGrid>

<ListBox icon="plus-circle" title="New Business">

- Duly accomplished Application Form
- DTI Certificate of Business Name Registration (for sole proprietorship)
- SEC Registration (for corporation/partnership)
- CDA Registration (for cooperatives)
- Barangay Business Clearance
- Proof of business location, such as a lease contract, or the title or tax declaration if you own the place
- Valid ID of the owner or authorized person

</ListBox>

<ListBox icon="arrow-repeat" title="Renewal">

- Previous year's business permit
- Declaration or proof of gross sales or receipts for the past year
- Barangay business clearance for the current year
- Updated clearances your city or municipality asks for

</ListBox>

</RouteGrid>

</Section>

<Section alt icon="building" title="What the Provincial Government Handles">

<ListBox>

- Under the Local Government Code, permits to extract sand, gravel and other quarry resources are issued by the Provincial Governor.
- Provincial taxes and fees are paid at the Provincial Treasurer's Office. [Provincial Treasurer's Office](/service-details/provincial-treasurer)

</ListBox>

<SectionHeading icon="question-circle" title="Frequently Asked Questions" />

<RouteGrid>
  <InfoCard title="When do I renew my business permit?">
    Every year. Under the Local Government Code, local business taxes are due in January, with
    quarterly installments where the LGU allows them. Check your city or municipality's deadline and
    penalties.
  </InfoCard>
  <InfoCard title="Can I apply online?">
    Some cities and municipalities accept online applications through their own systems or an
    electronic Business One-Stop Shop. Ask your BPLO what it offers.
  </InfoCard>
  <InfoCard title="My business has branches in different towns.">
    Each city or municipality issues its own permit. A branch or outlet usually needs a permit from
    the LGU where it operates.
  </InfoCard>
  <InfoCard title="Where do I register my business name?">
    Sole proprietors register the business name with the DTI. Corporations and partnerships register
    with the SEC, and cooperatives with the CDA.
  </InfoCard>
</RouteGrid>
