<Notice icon="info-circle" title="Kinsay nag-isyu og business permit">
  The Provincial Government of Bukidnon does not issue business permits for stores, offices or other
  establishments. Permits (often called the Mayor's Permit) are issued by the Business Permits and
  Licensing Office (BPLO) of the city or municipality where the business is located: Malaybalay
  City, Valencia City, or one of the 20 municipalities. Requirements, fees and deadlines vary, so
  confirm with that office. [Pangitaa ang imong siyudad o munisipyo](/government#lgus)
</Notice>

</Section>

<Section icon="clipboard-check" title="Kasagarang Gipangayo nga mga Rekisitos" intro="Each city and municipality publishes its own list. Use these to prepare, then confirm with your BPLO.">

<RouteGrid>

<ListBox icon="plus-circle" title="Bag-ong Negosyo">

- Hingpit nga napunan nga Application Form
- DTI Certificate of Business Name Registration (para sa sole proprietorship)
- SEC Registration (para sa korporasyon/partnership)
- CDA Registration (para sa kooperatiba)
- Barangay Business Clearance
- Proof of business location, such as a lease contract, or the title or tax declaration if you own the place
- Balidong ID sa tag-iya o awtorisadong tawo

</ListBox>

<ListBox icon="arrow-repeat" title="Pag-renew">

- Business permit sa miaging tuig
- Deklarasyon o pamatuod sa gross sales o resibo sa miaging tuig
- Barangay business clearance sa karon nga tuig
- Updated nga mga clearance nga gipangayo sa imong siyudad o munisipyo

</ListBox>

</RouteGrid>

</Section>

<SectionHeading icon="question-circle" title="Mga Kasagarang Pangutana" />

<RouteGrid>
  <InfoCard title="Kanus-a ko mag-renew sa akong business permit?">
    Every year. Under the Local Government Code, local business taxes are due in January, with
    quarterly installments where the LGU allows them. Check your city or municipality's deadline and
    penalties.
  </InfoCard>
  <InfoCard title="Makaaplay ba ko online?">
    Some cities and municipalities accept online applications through their own systems or an
    electronic Business One-Stop Shop. Ask your BPLO what it offers.
  </InfoCard>
  <InfoCard title="Ang akong negosyo adunay sanga sa lain-laing lungsod.">
    Each city or municipality issues its own permit. A branch or outlet usually needs a permit from
    the LGU where it operates.
  </InfoCard>
  <InfoCard title="Asa ko magrehistro sa ngalan sa akong negosyo?">
    Sole proprietors register the business name with the DTI. Corporations and partnerships register
    with the SEC, and cooperatives with the CDA.
  </InfoCard>
</RouteGrid>
