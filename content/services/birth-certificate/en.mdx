<Notice icon="info-circle" title="Who handles birth certificates">
  Births are registered with the Local Civil Registry Office (LCRO) of the city or municipality
  where the child was born: Malaybalay City, Valencia City, or one of the 20 municipalities of
  Bukidnon. The Provincial Government does not register births or issue birth certificates.
  Requirements and fees vary by city and municipality, so confirm with the office before you go.
  [Find your city or municipality](/government#lgus)
</Notice>

</Section>

<Section alt icon="files" title="Two Ways to Get a Copy" intro="Choose the office copy or the PSA copy, depending on what the requesting agency needs">

<RouteGrid>

<ListBox icon="building" title="From the City or Municipal Civil Registrar">

1. Go to the civil registrar of the city or municipality where the birth was registered.
2. Fill out the request form: full name, date and place of birth, and parents' names.
3. Present a valid ID. A representative also brings an authorization letter and the owner's ID.
4. Pay the fee set by the LGU and claim the certified copy.

</ListBox>

<ListBox icon="globe" title="From the PSA">

1. Order online through PSA Serbilis for delivery, or visit a PSA Civil Registry System outlet.
2. Give the owner's full name, date and place of birth, and parents' names.
3. Present a valid ID if you go in person, and pay the PSA fee.
4. Receive the copy printed on PSA security paper.

<ListNote>[PSA Serbilis (online requests)](https://www.psaserbilis.com.ph/)</ListNote>

</ListBox>

</RouteGrid>

</Section>

<Section icon="list-ol" title="Registering a Birth" intro="General steps. Your civil registrar may ask for more.">

<ProcessCards>
  <ProcessCard title="Get the Certificate of Live Birth">
    The hospital, clinic, midwife or birth attendant usually prepares the Certificate of Live Birth.
  </ProcessCard>
  <ProcessCard title="Check the Details">
    Check the spelling of names, the date and place of birth, and the parents' details before
    signing. Errors are harder to fix later.
  </ProcessCard>
  <ProcessCard title="Submit Within 30 Days">
    File it with the civil registrar of the city or municipality where the child was born, within 30
    days of the birth.
  </ProcessCard>
  <ProcessCard title="Add Supporting Documents">
    If the parents are not married, the father's acknowledgment and an Affidavit to Use the Surname
    of the Father may be needed. Ask the civil registrar.
  </ProcessCard>
  <ProcessCard title="Pay Any Fees">
    Pay any fee the city or municipality charges at its treasurer's office.
  </ProcessCard>
  <ProcessCard title="Get Your Copies" final>
    The civil registrar can issue a local copy once the birth is registered. The record is then sent
    to the PSA, so a PSA copy is available only after some time.
  </ProcessCard>
</ProcessCards>

<ListBox icon="person" title="For a Certified Copy">

- Valid government-issued ID of the requester
- Full name, date and place of birth, and parents' names on the record
- For a representative: authorization letter and the owner's valid ID

</ListBox>

<ListBox icon="clock-history" title="For Delayed Registration (After 30 Days)">

- PSA negative certification (proof that PSA has no record of the birth)
- Affidavit explaining the reason for the delay
- Supporting documents showing the name and birth details, such as a baptismal certificate or school records

</ListBox>

<SectionHeading icon="question-circle" title="Frequently Asked Questions" />

<Faq question="Does the Provincial Capitol issue birth certificates?">
  No. Birth records are kept by the civil registrar of the city or municipality where the birth was
  registered, and by the PSA. The Provincial Government of Bukidnon does not issue birth
  certificates.
</Faq>

<Faq question="I was born in Bukidnon but live somewhere else. How do I get a copy?">
  Order a PSA copy online through PSA Serbilis or at any PSA outlet. For a local copy, contact the
  civil registrar of the city or municipality where you were born.
</Faq>

<Faq question="What's the difference between a local copy and a PSA copy?">
  Both are certified. PSA copies on security paper are usually required for passports, visas and
  many national agencies. Local copies are often accepted for local transactions. Ask the agency
  which one it needs.
</Faq>

<Faq question="PSA says it has no record of my birth. What now?">
  Ask the civil registrar where the birth was registered to check its records. If the record is
  there, the office can endorse it to the PSA. If the birth was never registered, you will need to
  file a delayed registration.
</Faq>

<Faq question="How do I correct an error on my birth certificate?">
  Clerical errors, a change of first name, and errors in the day or month of birth or in sex can be
  corrected by petition at the civil registrar under Republic Act 9048 and Republic Act 10172. Other
  changes need a court order.
</Faq>

</InfoMain>
